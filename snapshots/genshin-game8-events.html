Battle Pass - Frostfarer

Availability Period August 12 - September 21, 2026
How to Unlock Reach Adventure Rank 20+

Participate in the free Sojourner's Battle Pass and the for-purchase Gnostic Hymn to receive prizes as you increase your BP Level.

Players who reach Gnostic Hymn Level 30 will also receive a 4-star weapon of choice as a BP Bounty!

Battle Pass Guide and All Weapons

Permanent Events

Travelers' Tales

How to Unlock Reach Adventure Rank 28+
Complete "The Many Matters Learned in One's Travels" World Quest

Travelers' Tales is a permanent character interaction game mode added in Version 6.0 Luna I, where players can find characters in the open world and get unique interactions!

Travelers' Tales Event Guide

Repertoire of Myriad Melodies

How to Unlock Reach Adventure Rank 35+
Complete "To the Winds of Freedom" World Quest

Repertoire of Myriad Melodies is a permanent rhythm game mode added in Version 5.3, where players can choose amongst a variety of instruments and different soundtracks to play from the game!

Repertoire of Myriad Melodies Guide

Daily Check-In

Similar to a Web Event

Receive daily login rewards by having your account registered at the HoYoLAB Community!

Daily Check-In Event Guide

Stellar Reunion


Stellar Reunion is an event for returnee players to Genshin Impact. Players who have not logged for at least 14 days and have not activated Stellar Reunion for at least 45 days are eligible to participate.

Rewards
Primogem Fragile Resin Hero's Wit

Stellar Reunion Returnee Event Guide

Fate Foretold

The Fate Foretold listing in the Events screen allows players to check upcoming Characters and Weapons which can be obtained from Quests!

Rewards
Lisa Kaeya Prototype Rancour Favonius Warbow

People's Choice

How to Unlock Reach Adventure Rank 20

The People's Choice Event offers the option to unlock the 4-Star Character Xiangling as long as you complete Floor 3 Chamber 3 of Spiral Abyss.

How to Raise Your Adventure Rank

Curtain of Leaves

Reach Floor 4, Chamber 3 to Unlock Collei

How to Unlock Reach Adventure Rank 20

The Curtain of Leaves event offers the option to unlock the 4-Star Character Collei!To get a free copy of Collei, beat Spiral Abyss at least up to Floor 4, Chamber 3.

Spiral Abyss Guide
