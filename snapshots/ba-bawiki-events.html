Story Events

[edit]

Story events are the most content-rich event type, typically including a tab for narrative, basic and challenge stages, along with a variety of shops and other reward mechanics if not full minigames. Many, but not all, events reward event-limited characters upon completing the first chapter or the entire story.

Typical lifecycle for an event is to be rerun once, roughly a year after original release, and to be added to a permanent event archive the following year. Permanent versions only retain the story section of the event and allow players to still acquire the event-exclusive limited character (assuming they were available as a reward), whose upgrade elephs will also be made available in the Mastery Certificate Shop at the same time.

Due to the nature of the main story, Some events may be seen as relevant in between certain chapters and may need to be experienced before certain main story chapters and volumes (Such as Volume F, Volume 3-3 or Volume 5, etc) to land their full impact. For a proper timeline of events, please see this steam guide.

Special Operation

[edit]

Special Operations was a recurring irregularly-held event type. Each new rerun would advance further parts of a developing, singular story, with re-runs appearing inconsistently compared to the annual nature of normal story events.

Note: Special Operations: Decagrammaton has since then removed from the Event Recap menu and transferred into main story since Serenade Promenade update (JP)/Sheside Outside update (Global).

Decagrammaton

[edit]

What exactly is "Decagrammaton", an autonomous analysis system for the Absolute?

To discover its secret, Eimi introduces Sensei to Himari from the Supernatural Phenomenon Task Force, and together they begin investigation to unveil the secrets of the unprecedented AI, Decagrammaton, rising in Kivotos.

Due to the original add-on nature of the story for each rerun, the story of Decagramation takes place all over separate parts of canonicity:

  • Episodes 1-12 take place after Volume 2, Chapter 1
  • Episodes 13-17 takes place after Volume 2, Chapter 2, as well as after Volume F

Note: All episodes have been transferred to Chapter 1 of Decagrammaton. Canonicity still applies.
