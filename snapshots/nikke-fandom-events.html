List of Events[]

\n

Story Events[]

\n

These are the primary limited-time events of the game which usually tells the story of the featured Pickup Recruit Banner Nikkes.\n

Story Events have three formats:\n

\n
  • The first one is the primary \"Normal\" type, which is the format for most events. Rarely, they have a Story II or voice-overs.
    • \n
  • The second one is the \"Grand\" type, which is often considered to be Major Events. These events have a Story II and quasi-open world map where the players can fully explore and find Lost Relics, as well interact with the map. Sometimes, these type of events have voice-overs in the cutscenes.
    • \n
  • The third one is the tertiary type, otherwise known as Event Quests. These events have a shorter lifespan compared to the previous two, hence they are often minor story events. They are formatted like the Story Campaigns or Commissions. These events often aren't featured with an associated Pick Up Recruit.
\n

Event Archiving[]

\n

Starting with the School of Lock update, previous events began to be permanently added to the Archives system, accessible via the Command Center of the Outpost. These \"Archived\" events lack the currency, shop, Challenge Mode, mission systems, and other version-exclusives (Special Recruit banners, event Mission Passes, etc.), but otherwise allow players to freely access and play the event stages and story without ticket limits or arbitrary time-gating. Event mini-games are implemented as well, either in the Archive itself or, as of the Old Tales update, the Arcade.\n

New events are added to the Archives with every update.\n

Unlocking an archived event costs one Memory Film which can be obtained during current Events with event missions and event shops.\n
