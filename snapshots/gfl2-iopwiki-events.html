Overview of all events for Girls' Frontline 2: Exilium.

Sunborn commented that a general one-year gap would be enforced between the content in CN and other servers,[1] but in actuality the sequence of events is heavily reordered to accommodate the rewriting of the story of early CN events. It was later stated that the release schedule would accelerate and eventually become synchronized for all regions.[2][3]

Events marked Neural Migration contain an extra “Neural Recollection” story.

Starting with Qiuhua's event in CN and Yoohee's event in EN, some events contain a map with a gift box that must be manually reached and open.

Commander, I've been working on a new composition recently, but for some reason, even though many melodies resonate in my mind, what ends up on the score are just chaotic, discordant notes.
My fellow band members suggest that my creative block might be due to focusing too intently on composing. They think that going out more might help me break through this impasse. I've been browsing information about nearby satellite cities and come[sic] across quite a few interesting events. There's even an exhibition that aligns perfectly with my composition theme.
So... I hope I'm not being too forward, but I'd like to extend an invitation. Would you be willing to join me on this inspirational outing? It might require adjusting your work schedule, but I would truly cherish the opportunity to explore with you.

Thanks to our inspiration-gathering trip together, Commander, I've finally completed the piece that had been stalled for so long.

When I played this new composition for everyone, they all said it made them feel bright, warm, and happy. Just like when I'm with you, Commander. I believe this is because of our time together that day. I've now recorded this piece and attached it to this message.
I wonder, Commander, what will you hear in this composition we created together?

ElmoMart

[edit]

Daily missions and reward ladder played from the Crew Deck. Harvest Vouchers are acquired by completing daily missions and can be used to buy various in-game resources within a supply batch. Players can choose not to buy certain items within a batch before completing their order and moving on to the next batch. Buying items from a certain supplier will unlock extra items from this supplier's rewards ladder.

This was the first event to run on international servers first instead of the CN server.
