Haunted Beach Shack Summer Event Part 2

Start Date July 30, 2026
End Date August 13, 2026
Rewards
・ Meta Jewels
・ Beachwear
・Haunted Beach Shack
・Summer Chigusa: Great Success
・Everlasting Incense
・Vicissitude Ink
・Gold Ticket
・Kagura Bell
・ Unknown Orange Revelation S (Revelation)
・Money

The Haunted Beach Shack Summer Event Part 2 went live on July 30, 2026 during the Version 4.6 update. Players will be given until August 13, 2026, to progress through the event and claim all of its rewards. Activities in the event include a Sea Fishing Cruise and a Boss Battle against Sahimochi-no-Kami.

Haunted Beach Shack Part 2 Event Guide

1st Anniversary Celebration

Start Date June 25, 2026
End Date July 30, 2026
Rewards Meta Jewels, Platinum Milicon, Joy Medal, Everlasting Incense, Vicissitude Ink, Resonating Heart Badge, Miracle Concert 5 Badge

June 26, 2026 marks the first anniversary for the Global and Japanese versions of Persona 5: The Phantom X!

In celebration of reaching this milestone, players are set to receive multiple rewards through various minigames and activites. This lasts for over a month, so make sure to take the time to collect all the rewards and freebies!

1st Anniversary Campaign and Event

Log-in Campaigns

Collaboration Event 1st Anniversary

Both the Collaboration event featuring Hatsune Miku, and the 1st Anniversary events, feature log-in campaigns that lasts for over 12 days.

Each campaign will feature a total of 10 Platinum Tickets and 5 Platinum Milicoins, and they will be running simultaneously! Make sure to check in on them to receive a grand total of 20 Platinum Tickets and 10 Platinum Milicoins!

Daily Login Bonus Event

Take Your Heart Event

The Take Your Heart Newcomer Event has 5 different sub-events under it — the Thieves' Alliance, Novice Thief Support (Banner), Converging Destinies, Crossroads of Fate, Thieves' Exchange, and Cafe Leblanc. These sub-events will have different rewards and ways to do them, so be sure to check out each one!

This event is only available for a month after starting your journey in P5X, so try to progress on this and get all the rewards before it disappears.
